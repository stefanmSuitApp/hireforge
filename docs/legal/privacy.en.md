## What we collect

**Every account:** email address, password (we store only a cryptographic hash, never the password), account role (candidate, employer, staff), the date the account was created, and confirmations of your email address and of accepting the terms of use.

**Candidates:**

- name, phone, a short headline and city, if you enter them;
- the CV you build on Šljakam: work experience, education and skills;
- CV files you upload (PDF, DOC or DOCX, up to 5 MB) and CVs you generate;
- job applications: the job, the CV you attached, a cover letter if you write one, and the application's status;
- saved jobs and saved searches.

**Employers:** company details (name, tax ID, registration number, address, bank account), billing contact (name, email, phone), the responsible person and their position, job ads, and the history of packages, proformas, invoices and payments.

**Technical data:** to protect the site from abuse (for example password guessing) we briefly record IP addresses together with the number of sign-in, sign-up and similar attempts. These records delete themselves after at most one hour (for clicks on an external apply link, after at most seven days and without an IP address). We use no analytics or advertising tools.

## Why we use it, and on what basis

- **Performing a contract** (Article 12(1)(2) of the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection; Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): opening and running your account, applications and passing them to the employer, publishing job ads, packages and payment, and account emails (email confirmation, password reset, notices about applications and job ads, proformas and invoices).
- **Legal obligation** (point 3; GDPR 6(1)(c)): keeping accounting documents (proformas, invoices, credit notes) and the data they require.
- **Legitimate interest** (point 6; GDPR 6(1)(f)): security and abuse prevention, database backups, handling disputes.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. We make no automated decisions about you.

## Who sees your data

- **The employer you apply to** sees what is in that application: your email address, the CV you attached and your cover letter, if you write one. Other employers do not see your data; Šljakam has no candidate database for employers to search.
- **Šljakam staff** (moderators and administrators) access data only as needed for support, checking job ads and billing.
- **Processors** that provide technical services to us, under contract and only on our instructions:
  - Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany: servers, database, files and backups (data centre in Nuremberg);
  - Resend, Inc., USA: sending email (your email address and the message).
- **Public authorities**, when the law requires it.

The site's content is edited in the Sanity CMS; no user data is stored there.

## How long we keep it

- **Account and profile:** while the account is active.
- **Deleted account:** 30 days from the request (you can restore the account during that time), then permanent deletion.
- **Applications (with cover letters):** 24 months from the application, then personal data is removed and only numbers (statistics) are kept.
- **CV files:** while they belong to an application or the account, at most as long as the application.
- **Proformas, invoices, payments and company details:** 10 years (statutory).
- **Record of staff actions:** indefinitely.
- **Database backups:** up to 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your data and a copy, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To delete your account, or for anything else, write to the address under "Who is the controller". We reply without delay and within 30 days at the latest.

If you believe the processing breaks the law, you can complain to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11000 Belgrade, [www.poverenik.rs](https://www.poverenik.rs).

## Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the site needs to work; that needs no consent.

- **Sign-in cookies** (`hf_candidate_access`, `hf_candidate_refresh`, `hf_access`, `hf_refresh`, `hf_staff_access`, `hf_staff_refresh`): keep you signed in; the short ones last 14 minutes, the long ones 14 days.
- **`NEXT_LOCALE`**: remembers your language.
- **Browser storage:** theme (light/dark), recently viewed jobs, whether you closed the sign-up bar, and an unfinished employer sign-up (so a page refresh doesn't lose it; removed once sign-up succeeds).

We use no analytics or advertising cookies. If we ever add them, we will ask for your consent first.

## Security

The site works only over HTTPS, passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes, only authorised staff can access data, and the database is backed up daily.

## Minors

Candidate accounts are for people aged 15 or over.
